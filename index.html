SERVICES

What we offer

Мокрий ефект, коричневі вії та вигини L / CC / D — без доплати.

  1. Brow Shaping

    Корекція / фарбування брів

    Precise shaping that honours your natural bone structure, followed by tinting for depth and definition. Each appointment opens with a personal consultation.

    30–45 хв від 300 ₴

  2. Brow Lamination

    Ламінування брів

    A restructuring treatment that lifts, sets and conditions brow hairs into a fuller, brushed-up silhouette lasting 6–8 weeks. Combined with shaping and tinting for a complete result.

    60–75 хв від 650 ₴

  3. Eyelash Extensions

    Нарощування вій

    Natural-looking classic, 2D–5D volume or trending sets (lami effect, jellyfish, wet lash, liner). Wet effect, brown lashes and L/CC/D curls included at no extra cost.

    2–3 год від 800 ₴

  4. Lash Lamination

    Ламінування вій + фарбування

    A lifting and tinting treatment that makes natural lashes appear longer, darker and beautifully curled. No extensions — just your own lashes, elevated.

    60 хв 800 ₴
